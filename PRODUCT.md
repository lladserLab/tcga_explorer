# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Computational biologists, translational researchers, bioinformaticians and
reviewers who explore TCGA transcriptomic survival associations. They work
primarily on desktop screens, compare many methodological choices, and need to
understand exactly how each result was produced.

## Product Purpose

TCGA-TRACE combines endpoint-aware survival analysis, Cox diagnostics, RMST,
cutpoint sensitivity, pan-cancer concordance and patient-level provenance in one
reproducible workspace. Success means a researcher can move from a biological
hypothesis to an interpretable result, inspect its assumptions and export enough
evidence for independent reproduction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, sober and transparent. The product should feel like a trusted
scientific instrument: modern and responsive, but never theatrical or
clinically authoritative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, marketing-style hero layouts, hospital or
electronic-health-record visual language, neon genomics aesthetics, decorative
card grids, gratuitous motion and interfaces that hide statistical assumptions
behind simplified scores.

## Design Principles

1. Put methodological context beside the decision it affects.
2. Make data and evidence the visual foreground.
3. Preserve calm hierarchy despite analytical density.
4. Use motion only to communicate state, location or completion.
5. Make provenance, limitations and uncertainty visible and exportable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must remain keyboard operable with visible
focus, sufficient contrast and touch targets, reduced-motion support, semantic
labels and status cues that do not rely on color alone. Scientific plots and
tables must remain understandable under common forms of color-vision
deficiency and at responsive desktop and mobile widths.
