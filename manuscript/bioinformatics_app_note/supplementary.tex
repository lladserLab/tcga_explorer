\section*{Supplementary Benchmark Reproduction}

The benchmark artifacts in this supplement were generated from a running local
TCGA Explorer stack at \texttt{http://localhost:3000/tcga\_explorer}. The
single-gene cutpoint suite can be reproduced with:

\begin{verbatim}
scripts/publication/run_single_gene_benchmark_suite.py
\end{verbatim}

The feature-level workflow benchmarks can be reproduced with:

\begin{verbatim}
scripts/publication/run_feature_benchmarks.py
\end{verbatim}

Tracked benchmark outputs include compact API responses, summary tables,
endpoint metadata, audit hashes and patient-record SHA-256 values. Full audit
bundles and patient-level exports remain local analysis artifacts and are not
tracked in git.

\section*{Supplementary Comparator Matrix}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Extended positioning against representative survival-analysis
resources.}
\label{tab:supp-comparator-matrix}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lXX}
\toprule
Resource & Confirmed strengths & Gap targeted by TCGA Explorer \\
\midrule
GEPIA2 & TCGA/GTEx expression, survival maps, gene/isoform/signature analysis
and API support. & Patient-level audit bundle, PH diagnostics, RMST and
cutpoint robustness as exported outputs. \\
cSurvival & Two genomic predictors, optimal cutoffs for two continuous
predictors, gene-set survival and cell-line integration. & Cited as prior art;
TCGA Explorer emphasizes endpoint provenance, auditability, PH diagnostics,
RMST and robustness panels. \\
DoSurvive & Multi-omic biomarker survival, log-rank, Cox, AFT, four survival
endpoint types and combined effects. & TCGA Explorer does not claim uniqueness
in multivariable survival; it adds hashable patient-level provenance and
RMST/cutpoint robustness outputs. \\
PESSA & ssGSEA pathway activation scores, median/optimal cutoffs, Cox models,
238 datasets, 51 cancer types and 13 survival outcomes. & TCGA Explorer uses
simpler auditable signatures and adds sample-selection audit, endpoint QC,
PH diagnostics and RMST. \\
UALCAN & TCGA/CPTAC expression, subgroup expression and survival
visualizations. & Does not center the workflow on exported survival provenance
and model diagnostics. \\
KM Plotter & Large-scale biomarker survival screening across mRNA, miRNA,
protein and DNA assays. & Screening-oriented; not centered on TCGA-CDR endpoint
provenance, PH diagnostics, RMST or cutpoint robustness. \\
UCSC Xena & Flexible cancer genomics browser, data hubs and Kaplan-Meier
survival analysis. & Reproducibility depends on manual user workflow rather
than a single exported audit object. \\
cBioPortal & Broad cancer genomics exploration, group comparisons and survival
analysis. & General portal rather than a transcriptomic survival robustness and
audit workflow. \\
TCGAbiolinks & Programmable TCGA data acquisition and analysis workflows. &
Requires coding; TCGA Explorer standardizes an interactive audit artifact. \\
TCGAplot & R package for TCGA pan-cancer analysis and visualization. &
Programmable package rather than a web workflow with a survival robustness
contract and exported audit bundle. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
